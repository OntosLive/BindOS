# Terminology

## Scene
An active interaction contour placed in a concrete context.

## Pattern
A recurring topology of relations that can recur across different contents and people.

## Gate
A condition controlling accessibility of a transition.

## Sanction
A consequence that changes the effective cost or admissibility of a move.

## MetaGate
Whether rules themselves may be named, questioned, or renegotiated.

## ExitGate
Whether a participant may leave or change the frame.

## Interpreter attack
A move that relocates structural contradiction into alleged defect of the decoder.

## Recursion
A repair or exit attempt is consumed as new evidence by the same pattern.

## Breakpoint
A structural modification that makes a previously unreachable non-trapped transition reachable.
